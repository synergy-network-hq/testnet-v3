# Canary protected accounts: decision memorandum

**Disposition: unratified engineering review. Do not schedule activation.** The 63-item [decision register](decision-register-v1.tsv) distinguishes source extraction, established rules, proposed assignments and incomplete tests. [SHA256SUMS](SHA256SUMS) identifies this review snapshot. A later complete activation packet needs new hashes and a decision from a body whose authority has been documented. Do not sign these draft bytes as the deployable protocol.

1. **Action namespace and proof framing.** Recommend the separate PW13 u16 proposal 28673–28682, strict RFC 8949 deterministic encoding, and an independently activated encrypted processing content profile. Accept as engineering assignments only. Freeze the content binding/outer admission proof, status codes and exact client/gateway bytes after implementation and encrypted interop. Existing APQE and Wallet cannot use this as a network submission contract.
2. **Fees and resource limits.** Recommend fully paid, permissionless commit/reveal sponsorship, separate payer scope and nonce, no sponsor ownership, no first-version subsidy. Use the existing fee formula but derive per-action gas, validity horizon and storage/pruning expectations from qualified independent-host cost measurements. Do not approve fictional numbers. Complete auth, paid revert and abuse tests.
3. **DKG and EVM residual authority.** Recommend advancing exact DKLs23 engineering candidate only for separate review and physical-device qualification. If symmetric 2-of-3 is adopted externally, explicitly approve and disclose that R plus either other share can sign for that EVM point outside Synergy. If that is unacceptable, keep this DKG profile disabled and require a reviewed asymmetric alternative. Native R remains recovery-only in every case. Pin the exact qualified binaries and authenticated semantic profile separately; establish verifiable DKG and continuity evidence.
4. **Policy and recovery.** Recommend the 7,200/172,800 consensus-time state machine in the companion, unchanged native spend while pending, replaced-role veto or D/P cancellation strictly before activation, no nonce reset and unfrozen native/EVM rollout as separate explicit states. Ratify the deterministic eligible-item budget, notification semantics and old-share limits only after state-machine execution, rollback and independent lost-role tests.
5. **Formation and authority.** Recommend ordinary spend-authorized treasury at Genesis and delivery only after genuine protected enrollment. Independently approve treasury custody, enough separate initial liability for fees, and the exact Genesis artifacts. No proof found that this repository, a developer, or an existing Testnet signing root is the ratification authority for this package. Obtain instrument/delegation, named deciding body, date and vote/quorum/signature over the final hashes; scope to fresh Canary only. Physical cutover requires its own exact approval.
