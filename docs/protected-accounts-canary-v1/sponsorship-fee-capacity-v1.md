# Fee, sponsorship and capacity candidate

Core `synergy-execution/src/fees.rs:9–75` validates a nonzero base fee and collector, caps gas, and settles gasUsed × governed baseFeePerGas to the collector. There is no independent per-byte/per-proof/per-state charge in that code. `dispatcher/native.rs:6` fixes legacy transfer gas to 21,000; `services/execution.rs:72–73,1038–1040` uses 40 nWei/gas and collector `synf1pnchsrnyral0u9r65xusjrexuctfh465h06l` in the existing runtime: 840,000 nWei on successful legacy transfer. This is not a priced enrollment or a reserved Canary collector; the fixture verifies atomic spending under that test schedule.

Recommend permissionless fully paid sponsorship for commit/reveal only. Authenticate a valid ordinary `syna` payer by the same independently verified FN-DSA-root/ML-DSA-87 delegation mechanism and current finalized binding as an ordinary account, but with explicit sponsor signing scope. No protected user funds debit or sponsor ownership/recovery control. Proposed signed value = pure ML-DSA-87 over SHA3-512(ASCII("AEGIS/ENROLL-SPONSOR/v1") || exact sponsor-intent CDDL bytes), context `AEGIS.ENROLL.SPONSOR.V1`, with canonical authorization carrier and signature bytes in content. Bind current Genesis, action, exact action payload SHA3-256, payer address, current ledger nonce, max total fee and inclusive finalized-height expiry. Any sponsor may independently pay a reveal; no operator can revoke another party's paid commitment. Sponsor competition consumes the selected payer nonce only on charged inclusion; stale nonce, wrong signature, missing reserved fee or insufficient fee cap are pre-inclusion failures and take no funds. Do not refund unused cap (never debit it). Included authorized revert consumes exactly one payer nonce and computed fee; no partial enrollment write. Inclusion storage, fee, nonce, and account changes must be atomic. No free transaction or network subsidy in the proposed v1 scope. Wallet may select an optional service sponsor; the service may impose API limits unrelated to consensus.

Existing ETDAG controls include ciphertext size classes, governed max protected gas/bytes and outstanding nonce slots; current local admission additionally limits 1 MiB/request, 16 pending, 8 MiB pending and 256 receipts. They do not prove indefinite registry storage is safe. Enroll decode limit 24 KiB, policy decode 10 KiB; verify 3 ML-DSA-87 + 1 ECDSA ownership for reveal, plus independent DKG and sponsor proofs. Commit needs sponsor and storage only; transfer verifies two ML-DSA-87. Benchmark with authenticated Linux providers, actual DKG and signed-content sizes. Measure p50/p95/p99 fixed-path CPU, peak memory and exact committed bytes for current state, then at 10k and 100k weighted proposals, including invalid inputs and worst-case joins. Measure retained records and replay under all-at-once commitment expiry. Choose deterministic per-action gas weights and protected gas/byte/storage/prune budgets against the slowest eligible host. Set no consensus per-sponsor quota only once aggregate bounds and fees prove abuse resistance. If proof is insufficient, cap global occupied pending commitments/state growth by qualification and choose a separately reviewed subsidy profile only if needed. No numeric pricing or quotas are proposed from a Mac component timing or copied legacy sample. Do not activate enrollment until the schedule and attack-load evidence are immutable.

The engineering adapter proposes binding scope domain `AEGIS/ENROLL-SPONSOR/v1` and purpose `protected-enrollment-sponsorship`. It strictly decodes the eight-field signed sponsor intent, checks the authenticated finalized ordinary binding and current execution nonce, and uses the typed APQE pure-signature verifier. The isolated commit test proved atomic charge and insert, rejection without charge for tampering, stale nonce, wrong network, expiry or inadequate fee cap, and single-nonce/single-fee settlement for an authorized duplicate without changing the original commitment. Neither sponsored reveal dispatch nor public admission/receipt codecs are implemented by this test.
