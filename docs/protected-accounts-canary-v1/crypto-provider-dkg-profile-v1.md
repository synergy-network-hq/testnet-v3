## EVM threshold decision and R custody

Use secp256k1 only for EVM or the initial EIP-712 address claim. Verify the full canonical SEC1 point and exact typed claim (network/Genesis, nonce, expiry and enrollment commitment); derive the 20-byte account from Keccak-256(X||Y)[12..], then display through EIP-55. A point/signature and syntactically valid 32-byte DKG commitment do not prove threshold DKG or participant-to-policy correspondence.

- **Cb-mpc:** APQE `examples/threshold_enrollment_challenge.rs:101` labels the engineering bridge 0.2.2; runner `scripts/run-threshold-enrollment-challenge.py` and `IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md:119–127,168–174` document separate-process fresh DKG, D+P exact challenge and test R blob restore. The example uses a synthetic transcript commitment; no exact upstream immutable cb-mpc source selection, approved point-to-policy verifier or lost-D/P replacement was established here. The [upstream maintainer guidance](https://github.com/coinbase/cb-mpc) explicitly does not promise lost-blob reconstruction and does not support mobile.
- **DKLs23:** Candidate `0xCarbon/DKLs23` v0.6.2 at `beb6d3b066f870421e1707a22bd400b735e300d7`; the `dkls23-core` and `dkls23-secp256k1` archive SHA-256 values are `b2093ae0b1da361de3a9ec14d205dd6f1f92add8b8d2e4880f2763f787653c25` and `f103e547baa29315c8a0f1295fae72c9e4e7bf1d5c7c4b73d3d75cb79d90104d`. APQE `docs/threshold-replacement-candidate-review.md:1–64` traces genuine fresh DKG, lost D and P, masked repair, refresh, full-point continuity, interrupted pre-commit, and independent OpenSSL signatures. These were same-host separate processes with synthetic authorization and R wrapping, not physical custody/activation. Upstream [security requirements](https://github.com/0xCarbon/DKLs23/blob/beb6d3b066f870421e1707a22bd400b735e300d7/docs/security.md) govern authenticated transport, one-use signing material, erasure and durable counterparty exclusion after applicable OT errors.

Recommendation: pursue **DKLs23 as the single engineering continuity candidate** only if the authorized body accepts `AEGIS_EVM_SYMMETRIC_2OF3_V1` with explicit risk disclosure. Its ordinary product flow can require D+P, but **R plus either other role can sign an external EVM transaction**; neither native delayed recovery nor a new native epoch revokes compatible captured old shares, a leaked scalar, or old external permits. If governance requires a cryptographically recovery-separated EVM access structure, neither examined path qualifies: keep the combined profile unavailable until a separately vetted provider proves that structure and continuity.

The missing DKG evidence contract must bind an approved provider/access-structure ID, a unique attempt, authenticated network/Genesis and enrollment policy, an ordered map of independent D/P/R participant and credential/version references, exact provider transcript commitments and resulting role verifying shares, and identical full aggregate point. A transcript digest alone cannot establish correctness. Specify what validators can independently verify from public evidence or what specifically authorized attester roots would be required; **no such root is approved**. Reject forged/truncated/mixed transcripts, provider substitution, duplicated/swapped roles, stale or replayed sessions, malformed point or share correspondence, and a real malicious participant abort across physical devices. Production replacement must stage and authenticate PrepareEpoch/CommitEpoch, preserve original full point, forbid mixed epochs and signing pending shares, test crash before/after commit, and refuse activation on a changed point.

R custody baseline is AID §7.2/PW07 and Security AI-025. Independent 256-bit entropy represented by 24 BIP-39 words is a backup factor, not a spend root. Derive a 32-byte phrase factor using HKDF-HMAC-SHA256 with 32-byte salt and ASCII `AEGIS/RECOVERY-PHRASE/v1`; derive a 32-byte NFKD UTF-8 passphrase factor with Argon2id v1.3, 65536 KiB, t=3, p=4, independent 16-byte salt; derive 32-byte package key with HKDF-HMAC-SHA256 over the ordered concatenation and independent 32-byte package salt with ASCII `AEGIS/RECOVERY-PACKAGE/v1` plus canonical role/account/wallet/epoch information. AES-256-GCM uses a fresh 12-byte nonce and 16-byte tag, bounded authenticated header and R-only payload. APQE `recovery_package.rs:1–29,193–233,270` implements the KDF and independent vector; `recovery_package/custody.rs:95–117,183–262,446` is test-only. Require fresh physical R restore against an independently obtained public manifest, wrong-factor/tag/rollback tests, full key lifecycle and opaque custody. The KDF alone is not a shipped restore workflow.
